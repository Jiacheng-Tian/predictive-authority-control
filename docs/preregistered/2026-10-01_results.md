# Follow-up experiment results (2026-10-01)

All runs share the preregistered 120-episode paired grid (85 seen /
35 unseen) from `config/pac_v4.yaml`; paired effects use model-seed
level t(4) 95% CIs.  Preregistration: `2026-09-30_followups.md`.
The frozen v3 baseline and the 2026-09-23 v4 archive are untouched;
every number below comes from a new run directory.

## P0 — effect of the TD3 target-backbone decay fix

| arm (RMSE-3D, m)      | seen fixed | seen buggy* | unseen fixed | unseen buggy* |
|-----------------------|-----------:|------------:|-------------:|--------------:|
| Residual PAC (r1)     |     0.0656 |      0.0663 |       0.1539 |        0.1160 |
| WM Residual PAC (r1)  |     0.0663 |      0.0668 |       0.1450 |        0.1170 |

*buggy = archived 2026-09-23 run (backbone decayed to 0.0825x).

- Seen block: no meaningful change (the residual head contributes
  little in-distribution either way).
- Unseen block: the buggy run's apparent advantage was an artifact.
  During the buggy training the decaying backbone progressively
  squashed its predicted alpha toward mid-range, so the deployed policy
  behaved like a smoothed constant-alpha blend; that accidentally
  generalized well OOD.  With the correctly frozen backbone the
  preregistered comparisons lose significance:
  residual vs supervised, unseen mean difference -0.0073 m,
  t(4) CI [-0.0302, 0.0156]; seen +0.0021 [-0.0016, +0.0059].
- Honest conclusion for the manuscript: Residual PAC does **not**
  significantly beat Supervised PAC under the corrected protocol; the
  earlier claim must be retracted/corrected.  The WM hybrid arm keeps a
  small (also non-significant) unseen edge over the supervised
  baseline (-0.0020 m [-0.0072, +0.0031]).

## P1 — fixed-alpha sweep (RMSE-3D, m, 120-episode means)

| alpha | seen | unseen |
|------:|-----:|-------:|
| 0.25  | 0.0713 | **0.1183** |
| 0.50  | **0.0662** | 0.1206 |
| 0.75  | 0.0667 | 0.1788 |

Crossover pattern: higher authority blending helps in-distribution up
to alpha ~0.5 and collapses out-of-distribution beyond 0.5.  Fixed
alpha 0.5 is the strongest unseen method overall.  Figure:
`paper/generated_figures_v4/fig_v4_f6_alpha_sweep.*`.

## P2 — round-2 partial unfreeze (encoder_last + head)

Continued each of the five fixed round-1 checkpoints for another 200k
steps with exactly `encoder.layers[-1]` + `backbone.head` unfrozen
(acceptance gate: all never-unfrozen tensors bitwise equal to the v3
baseline).  Formal run: `formal/round2-eval` (14 combos x 120).

| Residual PAC (RMSE-3D, m) | seen | unseen |
|---------------------------|-----:|-------:|
| round 1 (frozen backbone) | 0.0656 | 0.1539 |
| round 2 (partial unfreeze) | 0.0653 | 0.1471 |

- Paired round-2 vs round-1: seen -0.0003 [-0.0058, +0.0051];
  unseen -0.0068 [-0.0235, +0.0098] — a small non-significant OOD
  improvement (4/5 seeds improve), no ID change.
- Preregistered comparison vs the supervised backbone: unseen
  -0.0140 [-0.0462, +0.0181] — still not significant.
- Conclusion: adapting the last encoder block recovers nothing beyond
  noise under this budget; the frozen-backbone constraint is not the
  limiting factor.

## P3 — direct-RL negative control

| method (RMSE-3D, m) | seen | unseen |
|---------------------|-----:|-------:|
| Direct RL (all 5 seeds) | 3.59 | 3.50 |
| best other (SSPO / Fixed alpha 0.5) | 0.062 | 0.121 |

Every seed fails by ~50x (per-seed range 2.5-3.9 m).  Training rewards
(-39 .. -54 per step) match the evaluated error, i.e. this is what the
agent actually learned, not an evaluation mismatch.  Interpretation:
200k steps of model-free TD3 without the backbone, the alpha-blend
structure, and the safety stack cannot solve this tracking task — the
architectural prior carries the performance, supporting the paper's
central claim (structure > raw RL capacity).

Takeaway for the paper: after the P0 correction the adaptive-authority
story shifts from "RL residual improves the supervisor" to "the
supervised alpha stack already captures most of the achievable gain;
simple conservative blending is the strongest OOD policy; direct RL
without structure fails catastrophically".
